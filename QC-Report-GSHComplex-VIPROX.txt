2. GSH COMPLEX - "TWO INGREDIENTS" AS THE POSITIONING                     CLOSED

Rewritten along the line suggested in the review. Health Canada's licence

The real file is one path away and has been confirmed as the brochure:
19 pages, opening "Live GSH Complex for your immune system".

  Now:  yourcym.com/files/Booklet-GSHE-9avril.pdf   (3.9 MB, 19 pages)

11. CLINICAL STUDY DISCLAIMER - REFERENCES CANNOT BE LOCATED             BLOCKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This point is correct, and the reason is worse than a missing link.

page now also points readers to the Documentation section.

  NEEDED FROM CYM
  The citation for the 115-participant randomised, double-blind,
  placebo-controlled study. Journal and year, or the report PDF, plus the names
  of the two U.S. universities. Send it and it will be wired in the same day.

  Until then the study is described on the page with no reference behind it,
  which is exactly the exposure the review is pointing at.

PART 3 - ADVISORIES: THESE NEED A DECISION, NOT A CODE CHANGE
================================================================================

Nine items that were deliberately not changed without someone at CYM saying so.
Several are the kind of item that will come back on the next review if they go
untouched.


A. THE UNITED STATES LABEL SAYS SOY LECITHIN, THE PAGES SAY SUNFLOWER
   The supplement facts panel on 112222-011-RV0418-USAback reads
   "Soy lecithin / Lecithine de soya". The review email and both pages say
   sunflower. Soy is a declarable allergen, so this needs settling at source
   before either statement stays published. The pages have been left saying
   sunflower and the conflict flagged here rather than resolved by guesswork.

B. THE CANADIAN LABEL LINK OPENS A PRINTER'S APPROVAL PROOF
   GSHCOMPLEX-BACK-CAN_en.pdf is a pre-press sheet. It carries an
   "Epreuve d'approbation / Approbation proof" header, sign-off boxes reading
   "Approved (no corrections needed)", dieline and web-direction marks, and a
   customer ID. The label content is correct and legible, but a customer
   clicking "View label" is shown an internal production document. Worth
   requesting clean artwork from the printer.

C. THE BROCHURE'S COVER CARRIES A THIRD-PARTY BRAND
   Page one of the live brochure ends "www.nutraxis.com". The Michrowski
   scientific opinion is likewise addressed to Nutraxis International Inc.,
   not Cymcorp, and dated 2014. Both are the documents the review asked us to
   link, so they are linked, but a customer who reads them meets a different
   company's name.

D. THE MICHROWSKI OPINION MAY NOT BE OURS TO PUBLISH
   Its own first page states that reports are "the confidential property of
   submitter" and that authorisation for publication "is reserved pending our
   written approval". It is linked from the VIPROX page. Someone should
   confirm that approval exists.

E. THE GSH FORMULA PHOTOGRAPH CONTRADICTS THE NEW COPY
   Directly beside "One ingredient does the work: fresh whey" sits a flat lay
   of capsules, bright green powder, blueberries, seeds and dried botanicals.
   None of it is in GSH Complex, and the capsules imply a format the product
   does not come in. Now that the section makes a single-ingredient claim, the
   picture works against it. A shot prompt for a replacement can be supplied
   on request.

F. THE VIPROX PHOTO SHOWS A BOTTLE THE COPY DOES NOT DESCRIBE
   Une Vie appears in the box shot as a 32 fl oz "Blue Wave Balance" bottle.
   The page, and the real label, say a 500 ml concentrate. Separately, the two
   GSH pouches in that shot read "Immune Support" while the pouch on the GSH
   Complex page reads "Immune System": two versions of the pack artwork across
   two pages.

G. "98.9% EFFICACY" HAS NO SOURCE ON THE PAGE
   Stated as fact in the VIPROX protocol section: "Three elements working in
   synergy for 98.9% efficacy, which no single product can reach on its own."
   A precise percentage invites the question of where it came from, and the
   page cannot answer it. Same family of problem as the missing study
   reference.

H. AN UNUSABLE PRODUCT MOCK IS SITTING IN THE ASSETS FOLDER
   assets/hero-banner-mobile.jpg shows a pouch branded "GSH NUTRITION" rather
   than CYM, with garbled badge text and "60 Vegetarian Capsules / Dietary
   Supplement" on a product that is a 450 g powder. It is not referenced by
   either page, but it is one careless import away from being published.
   Recommend deleting it.

I. DEAD MARKUP AND PLACEHOLDER LINKS
   The GSH Complex page still carries the previous hero, roughly 150 lines,
   hidden with display:none. Harmless to customers but it ships on every page
   load. It can be deleted on request.

   There are also 24 href="#" placeholders per page: About Us, Terms, Refund
   Policy, the social icons. Every one of them is in the header, drawer or
   footer, which the live site replaces with its own, since only the page's
   main content block is deployed. No customer meets them, so destinations
   have deliberately not been invented for them.
