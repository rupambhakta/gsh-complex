# Two things still open for Robert

**1. The GSH Complex NPN.** The page currently shows **NPN 800021489** — nine digits, copied from the GSH Complex listing on yourcym.com. The Triozyme number Robert supplied, **80094773**, is eight digits, which is the correct Canadian NPN format, and the approved homepage shows **80021489** for GSH. The nine-digit version is almost certainly a typo on the live site. One character to fix, but it is a regulatory identifier so it needs Robert's confirmation rather than our assumption.

**2. Scope of "do not mention the 2 pouches."** The explanatory sentence on the GSH Complex card is gone as requested. The **quantity** "2 ×" still appears in the hero kit line, the contents list, the price breakdown and the FAQ, because $85.95 × 2 is what produces the $267.20 comparison. Remove the quantity everywhere and the $250 value argument no longer adds up. Confirm whether he meant the sentence only, or the quantity as well — if the latter, the price section needs rebuilding around a different comparison.

# Two points worth raising before publish

**The two short testimonials no longer match what the customers wrote.** Debbie J.'s timeframe changed from the fifth week to the third, and Martin N.'s "much healthier overall" became "vibrant and energised". Both are named, attributed, "Verified purchase" quotes. Canadian Competition Bureau rules and the US FTC endorsement guidelines both require an attributed testimonial to be the endorser's own statement. Three clean routes: source the customers' actual words saying this, get written sign-off on the revised wording, or drop the names and run the quotes unattributed.

**Removing the guarantee qualifier while adding "we guarantee!"** changes what the 45-day promise appears to cover — from a refund on the purchase to a health outcome. The equivalent wording still lives in the FAQ answer ("It is not a guarantee of a particular health result"), which is the only place it now appears.
