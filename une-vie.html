What suggestions do you have for someone considering Une Vie?

Staying well-hydrated is an important part of supporting overall wellness. We encourage users to prioritize clean, fresh water as part of their daily routine. Une Vie is designed to complement your hydration goals when used as directed, supporting your efforts to maintain healthy hydration habits.

How do I use Une Vie?

Une Vie is a concentrated solution and should always be diluted before use. Simply mix 1 teaspoon of Une Vie with 1 litre of spring water. We recommend enjoying at least two glasses of the mixture per day, or as part of your regular hydration routine.

For larger batches: 30 ml (1 oz) in 4 litres (1 gallon) of spring water, or 15 ml (½ oz) in 2 litres (½ gallon).

Can someone drink too much Une Vie™?

As with any concentrated supplement, more is not always better. Always use Une Vie as directed. If you're new to the product, it's best to start with the suggested amount to see how your body responds. Individual experiences may vary.

What exactly is structured water?

Une Vie is produced through a proprietary technology using excitation currents as well as quartz wavelength technology purification, which gives the water a unique proprietary structure. It acts as a highly efficient delivery system with powerful hydrating and restorative properties that transport vital nutrients to every cell in your body.

What is in it?

One ingredient: purified water, described on the label as a cellular hydrating solution. No sugar, no caffeine, no flavouring and no preservatives. Une Vie is licensed under Natural Product Number 80094773.

How should I store it?
