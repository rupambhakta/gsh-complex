Why buy the products as a package?

Prezerv Gold brings the whole range together in one box, so you can put the full routine in place without ordering five things and working out the quantities. It is also the only way to get everything at once at a single price.

How long does one box last?

The two GSH Complex pouches are the limit. Each holds 450 g, or forty-five 10 g scoops. At the maintenance dose of one scoop a day the two pouches are ninety days. On the 3-2-1 starter regimen they go a good deal faster, because the first fifteen days are three scoops a day.

Everything else outlasts them. The Une Vie bottle makes roughly a hundred litres of water, the Triozyme jar holds 180 tablets, and the PREZERV derma bottle is 30 ml used two dabs at a time.

Is it actually cheaper than buying the five?

By $16.08. The five come to $321.70 one at a time and the box is $305.62. The real saving is on volume: six boxes bring it to $273.45 each and twelve to $257.36.

Which of these carry Health Canada licences?

Two of them. GSH Complex® is licensed under NPN 80021489 and Triozyme® under NPN 80094773.

Une Vie™ has none because it is a natural spring water concentrate rather than a licensed natural health product, and PREZERV derma™ has none because it is a cosmetic. Neither absence is a gap; it is what those two products are.

Is there anyone who should not take it?

Yes. Anyone allergic to milk proteins should not take GSH Complex, which rules out the box as a whole. Anyone who has had an organ transplant and is on immunosuppressive therapy should speak to their doctor first. If you are pregnant, nursing or taking medication, check with your practitioner before starting.

Why can the GSH Complex not go in a blender?

A blender and a hot drink both disrupt its bioavailability, which is the part you are paying for. That is what the shaker in the box is for: stir it by hand into something cold and drink it within the hour.

What does the 45-day guarantee cover?

It covers your purchase. If you do not experience a positive difference within 45 days you will receive a full refund, no questions asked. It is a refund on what you paid, not a guarantee of a particular health result.
