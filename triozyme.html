Who may benefit from Triozyme?

Triozyme is a dietary supplement that provides digestive enzymes which support normal digestive function. It may be a beneficial addition to the diet of children, adults, seniors and pregnant individuals seeking to maintain healthy digestion as part of their daily routine.

Is Triozyme suitable for people with gluten sensitivities?

Triozyme contains a small amount of gluten, 15 to 200 parts per million, so individuals with gluten sensitivities should consult their healthcare provider before using this product.

How does Triozyme support digestive function?

Triozyme contains amylase, a digestive enzyme that helps break down carbohydrates. This supports the normal digestion of nutrients at various stages of the digestive process.

Can I take Triozyme while on other supplements or medications?

Triozyme is a dietary supplement intended to support digestion. If you are taking medications or have specific health concerns, we recommend consulting your healthcare provider before using this or any supplement.

How should I use Triozyme?

The suggested use is two tablets after each meal, or as directed by a healthcare professional. Individual needs may vary depending on dietary habits and wellness goals.

Chew the tablets rather than swallowing them whole. For occasional digestive challenges, chew one to two tablets as needed; during periods of dietary stress, chew four to six.

How long does one jar last?

One jar holds 180 chewable tablets. At the suggested use of two tablets after each of three meals, that is roughly a month, or about 33¢ a tablet at the standard price and less again with volume pricing.
